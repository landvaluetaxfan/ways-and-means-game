III The diagnosis

There are two games and they do not touch.

A — the narrative

  • events, choices, flags
  • prose and consequence
  • all 21 events live here
↔two thin wires

B — the parliament

  • whips, slots, divisions
  • instruments, appointments
  • nearly all the engineering
A player can finish Game A without meaningfully playing Game B. That is more fundamental than pacing, and it is why the loop feels thin when both halves are good.

The fix is one rule: every chapter puts commitments on the docket with a date, and the session’s order-paper time is the entire capacity to meet them. The player will not meet all of them. Which one they drop is the game.

A lobbying currency

One of the four settlements is currently unreachable. Substrate neutrality needs the dual majority; the dual majority needs the functional forty; and moving a bench outside the coalition is impossible by design — the whip panel says so in as many words. Part XVI has flagged this as an open decision and it is not a content gap, it is a correctness one: the engine offers a win the player cannot get to.

Wants its own currency, not capital. Capital is a debt between governing partners; this is something else, paid to people who owe you nothing.

Actors outside the chamber

Metanationals, the unions, the fork-rentiers and the licensing board have near-party power in the bible and no representation in state at all. Something that can be undone

An instrument can be revoked and a bill can be referred. Nothing reverses an Act, so the player’s mistakes are permanent and inert — which is a thriller without a second act. A court is the natural author of a reversal, and §10.8 has long-lived emulated judges who personally remember the founding sitting on it.

V Landed since the last revision

None of these were on the list above when it was written. Several came out of playing the thing rather than planning it.

WhatWhy it mattered
Order-paper time became a clock It capped how much you could do and said nothing about when: all six slots were spendable on sitting 1. The House now hears two divisions a day, and a measure must have had its second reading — so a drafting bill costs two grants and a division, half a session, and Grant is finally a step rather than a favour.
The queue carries a fact, not only a story Anything that happened later had to happen as an authored event, so a court could not return a verdict without prose written first. That was the thing standing between building the engine and writing content afterwards.

A division counts three ways, and pairs Aye, nay, abstain — and absent, once pairing landed. A pair costs the government an aye and the opposition a nay the threshold never counted, because a majority here is a majority of the members. A Westminster courtesy this chamber’s arithmetic does not support.

  • Not consumables. The first draft of this list said cut it. That was wrong: §7.2 is LOCKED and closure is the sovereignty number. A dead number is more often unwired than unwanted — check the wiring before reaching for the knife.

IX Settled in canon

§What it now says
3.5.1Four settlements — restriction, substrate neutrality, graduated personhood, the federal fudge. The player is never shown the list. Closure and dissolution are failure modes, not settlements.
7.7Order-paper time is the pacing instrument, not merely a scarce good. Everything the House does costs it. A free division is a bug.
1.7Four chapters. A campaign is one parliament and one parliament is one session. At the end of it the House is dissolved and the electorate answers, so the election is the campaign's terminus rather than an interruption — and the next campaign opens in the Commonwealth that answer produced. A run finishes three ways and cannot continue past them: a settlement, the election, or a loss.
4.6.1aDomain consent. A measure that touches an interest is answered by the functional constituencies that own it. It is consent and not endorsement — they must not oppose, they need not carry — and the House may override on three-fifths of those voting. Supply is exempt: the elected benches vote money, the forty are heard and not obeyed, and an objection delays it three sittings rather than killing it.
3.2Unicameral. No second chamber, decided rather than merely unanswered. The three tiers are already the revising mechanism: a dual majority makes a measure carry the popular benches and the functional forty separately, so a senate would duplicate that check and halve its legibility.
8.3A party is named for a value, an interest, a place or a founding event — almost never for its ideology, and never for what it opposes. Three parties renamed under it.

XI Since the hundred

The three that were built and unreachable are reachable. The run has a length.

LandedWhat it changed
The roll call A division names every member who cast it, party by party as the lobbies fill. District members have a name and a seat; functional members a register reference; list members a stable placeholder, because a list member still sits. The count climbs with it — the old build showed the names and then counted separately, two accounts of one event.
Pairing, reachable Exported since 15 Sep with no control in the interface calling it. Seven parties were pairable on a bill and the player could not discover one.
Abstention, used Counted three ways since 14 Sep and used by no bill. Two bills abstain now, so the count is exercised by play.
Lobbying, and ten actors The correctness hole item 10 named. A whip spends capital; a lobby spends a promise, so the bench is paid for next session through a real undertaking. Reach scales with standing and delivers into the constituencies the body actually holds. Substrate Neutrality is reachable — design/24 A2's acceptance criterion, asserted.
Domain consent Twenty-two authored interests that nothing read. Now the bench that owns the subject answers for it, with three guards so it is a price and not a veto — and an asserted invariant that no constituency is beyond every mechanism.
The parliament ends Four loss conditions and a settlement still left a run that could go on for ever. Dissolution, then the electorate. Empty sittings: 190 → 9–15, and every play policy now ends at sitting 25.
