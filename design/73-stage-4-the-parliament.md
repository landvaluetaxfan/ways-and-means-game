# 73 — Stage 4: the Parliament

**4 October 2026 (evening).** The author, after the assessment that the game was a
good vessel with an unproven loop: "let's try building leverage, witnessed acts,
and stage 4, by tonight. Then we can rewrite the opening, and start testing." This
record splits Stage 4 so that tonight builds the engine and the author's own
decisions are asked for the rest. It supersedes the exchange question "what ends
the built run": the answer is that it does not end early. The run is the
Parliament (design/58, bible §1.8), and a curtain at the end of Act I is dropped.

## What Stage 4 is

design/58 and bible §1.8 decided it: Flash I is the Parliament of 2080 to the
election of 2084, in five acts (the opening weeks day by day; three acts across the
middle years, each skipping time before it; the last year ending in the run-in and
the count); an act is a run of sitting days; an interval is read, then a course is
set; reshuffles happen at act breaks; the run-in carries preselection; the opposition
(Watkins) is a rival who acts, and his moves reach the player as matters; the
legacy reads promises, methods, injuries and alliances; the grid of twelve endings
is Flash I's size.

## Proposals for 4b, for the author to change

Dates are authored in content; these are starting points, not canon.
- **Act I** (spine and shape: design/76), April to August 2080, about 22 sittings: the existing opening, ending
  at the count-day set piece's replacement (a confidence test on the Divergence
  bill, in the summer), with the stranding at sitting 14 as built.
- **Acts II, III and IV**: the autumn sittings of 2081, 2082 and 2083, about 8
  sittings each, each with its fork (the courts; the UN; a tariff standoff with
  Earth; a retreat).
- **Act V**: the last year, from the new year of 2084, about 6 sittings, then the
  run-in (`setup.campaignSittings`, 12) and the count.
- Reshuffles at each interval; the interval reports what endured, what decayed and
  who used the absence.

## Open questions for the author (recommendations given)

1. **Is about 55 sittings right for the whole Parliament?** Recommended: yes
   (design/58 Round E), with Acts II to IV short and sharp.
2. **Does Act I end on a confidence test or on a calm interval?** Recommended: a
   test, so the first interval is earned.
3. **How much of Acts II to V is written before the opening is rewritten?**
   Recommended: skeletons only (one page per act: aim, fork, the settlement
   family it leans on), so the opening can be rewritten toward them without
   writing four years first.
