# Design records

Every file here is a **dated record** of a design decision and the
reasoning behind it. None of them is an instruction. The current rules are in
`AGENTS.md`, `CONTENT_GUIDE.md` and `PROSE.md`, and canon is in `bible.md`.
Where a record disagrees with those files, the record is history. Where two
records disagree, the later one wins.

Code comments cite these files by number ("design/43 §2"), so they are kept
as written and not renumbered. Add a new record at the next number when a
decision needs its reasoning kept; the current work itself goes in
`briefs/`.

## Archive

`archive/` holds documents that were once plans or status reports and have
been overtaken: the sweep brief, the gameplay-loop diagnosis, the roadmap,
the plain-text authoring format, and the bible's retired anthro setting.
They are kept for their reasoning, and they describe the project as it was
on the dates they carry.
